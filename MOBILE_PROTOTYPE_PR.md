# Add free exploration, collectible-star victory and three consecutive jumps

Reaching Ember previously ended the level. The mobile scene now permits landing anywhere on either planet and wins only after all three gold stars are collected, in any order. Two surface stars are on Azure and one on Ember. A visible counter tracks progress; checkpoint retries keep collected stars, while Play Again resets every pickup and restarts on Azure. No landing zone is added. Progress is in-session only.

Running remains available through the joystick or Shift, but no longer supplies escape height. Players chain three grounded jumps, tapping again within 1.2 seconds of each landing. Each jump is higher, and the third can escape the finite gravity field. Airborne taps cannot skip stages. Pause freezes the landing window; timeout, planet switching and retry reset it. The first two takeoffs compensate for running around a small sphere so speed cannot bypass the chain. The jump button shows the next stage and highlights the escape jump.

Validation covers an actual third-jump crossing without Shift, increasing heights and timing, airborne taps, pause/resume, lower running jumps on both planets, missed-jump defeat, real pickup triggers, any-order collection, duplicate and state guards, counter updates, checkpoint persistence, last-star victory and full replay reset. The existing rendered touch routing, safe-area, camera clearance and saved-scene checks remain. Build and test outputs are ignored by Git; the Android test artifact is Builds/MobilePrototype/GravityGame-Stars.apk. Phone feel and difficulty still need hands-on testing.

Local working branch: codex/collectible-stars. Publication branch: codex/mobile-star-exploration (a review snapshot containing the previously unpublished two-planet and mobile features).
